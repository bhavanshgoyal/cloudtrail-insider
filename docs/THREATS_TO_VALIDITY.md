# Threats to Validity

This section honestly identifies the limitations of our experimental setup and the claims it can and cannot support.

---

## 1. Internal Validity

### 1.1 Synthetic Data Bias
The entire evaluation runs on a **synthetic CloudTrail dataset** generated by our own engine ([`synthetic.py`](../src/cti/ingest/synthetic.py)), not from a real cloud environment. The four insider scenarios (S1: exfil-after-hours, S2: departing-insider, S3: privilege-escalation, S4: credential-stuffing) are hand-crafted attack patterns. Real insider behaviour is more diverse, subtle, and context-dependent. Results demonstrating detection improvements on synthetic data **may not generalise** to production CloudTrail logs with real adversarial behaviour.

### 1.2 Label Leakage Risk
Although we enforce causal windowing (14-day warmup, temporal train/test split at 2023-10-01), the synthetic generator plants malicious events at deterministic times derived from the random seed. There is a residual risk that temporal patterns embedded by the generator — such as the concentration of malicious events in the second half of the timeline — could inadvertently provide information leakage to models that exploit distributional shifts over time.

### 1.3 Single-Seed Preliminary Results
Current results are computed with a single random seed (42). The hypothesis H1 formally demands **5 seeds × 3 temporal splits** with BCa bootstrap confidence intervals for statistical rigour. The current evaluation is preliminary and the observed effect sizes may not be robust to seed variation.

### 1.4 Calibration Set Size
The conformal calibration step uses a 30% held-out split from the training data. For principals with few events, the calibration set may contain only a handful of scores, making the conformal p-values unreliable for low-activity users. This primarily affects human users with <50 events/day.

## 2. External Validity

### 2.1 Scale Limitations
Our testbed generates **1,000 principals over 180 days**, producing ~575 MB of event data. A real enterprise cloud environment may have **100,000+ identities** generating terabytes of audit logs daily. Performance characteristics — both computational cost and detection accuracy — at production scale are unknown. The per-principal Isolation Forest approach (C1) may not scale linearly with principal count.

### 2.2 Limited Event Vocabulary
The synthetic generator uses a vocabulary of approximately **15 API action types** (e.g., GetObject, ConsoleLogin, CreateAccessKey). Real CloudTrail logs contain **500+ distinct event types** across dozens of AWS services. The feature engineering pipeline's effectiveness on this broader event space has not been validated.

### 2.3 LocalStack ≠ Production AWS
The testbed uses **LocalStack Community Edition** to simulate cloud services. LocalStack does not implement the full CloudTrail `LookupEvents` API (this is a Pro feature). Our evidence proves the pipeline runs in a Docker-based cloud simulation environment, but not that it ingests live AWS CloudTrail streams or handles production-scale event delivery.

### 2.4 No Multi-Tenant Evaluation
Our dataset simulates a single AWS account (123456789012). Real organisations operate across multiple accounts, OUs, and even cloud providers. Cross-account insider activity and federated identity attacks are not modelled.

## 3. Construct Validity

### 3.1 Alert Budget Assumption
We assume a **fixed daily alert budget** (B ∈ {10, 50, 100}) as the correct operational constraint. In practice, SOC teams may use:
- Weekly or monthly review cycles rather than daily
- Severity-weighted queues where critical alerts bypass the budget
- Dynamic throttling based on analyst availability
- Automated triage that changes the effective budget

Our results hold under the strict "k alerts/day" model but may not apply to other operational paradigms.

### 3.2 SHAP on Baseline, Not Proposed Method
SHAP explanations are computed using `TreeExplainer` on the **Isolation Forest baseline**, not on the proposed BudgetAwareConformal detector directly. The BudgetAwareConformal pipeline involves per-principal IF models, conformal calibration, and Fisher fusion — none of which are directly amenable to TreeExplainer. The explanations are therefore **approximate** for the proposed method and attribute importance based on the global IF's feature sensitivities, not the per-principal scoring logic.

### 3.3 Ground Truth Granularity
The ground truth labels are at the **(principal, day)** level: any day where a principal generates at least one malicious event is marked positive. This means a day with 1 malicious event and 99 normal events is fully positive. This granularity may inflate or deflate detection metrics compared to event-level labelling.

## 4. Statistical Conclusion Validity

### 4.1 Bootstrap Resampling Unit
Our bootstrap confidence intervals resample individual **(principal, day)** rows independently. If there is **temporal autocorrelation** in anomaly scores — e.g., a principal flagged on Monday is likely to remain flagged on Tuesday — this resampling scheme may understate the true variance of our estimators. A block-bootstrap resampling entire days would be more conservative.

### 4.2 Multiple Comparisons
We evaluate 5 detectors across 3 budgets and 4 metrics, yielding 60 statistical comparisons. No family-wise correction (e.g., Bonferroni, Holm) is applied. Some observed differences may be statistically significant by chance.

### 4.3 Detector Hyperparameter Selection
Baseline detector hyperparameters (IF: 200 estimators, contamination=0.02; LOF: 20 neighbours) were set from the configuration file without systematic tuning. The proposed method's hyperparameters were also manually chosen. A fairer comparison would apply the same hyperparameter optimisation procedure to all detectors.

---

## Summary

| Threat Category | Key Risk | Mitigation Path |
|----------------|----------|----------------|
| Internal | Synthetic data ≠ real attacks | Validate on CERT r4.2 or real CloudTrail |
| Internal | Single seed | Run 5 seeds × 3 splits (H1 protocol) |
| External | 1K principals ≠ 100K | Profile computational cost at scale |
| External | LocalStack ≠ AWS | Deploy pilot on live account |
| Construct | SHAP on IF, not proposed | Implement KernelExplainer for proposed |
| Statistical | No multiple-comparison correction | Apply Holm-Bonferroni |
